When text is right vs when to go to the nearest ER

NPCWoods is for straightforward, non-emergency problems that are safe to evaluate by text when clinically appropriate.

Text care may fit for:

  • UTI symptoms
  • Sinus infection
  • Strep throat
  • Ear infection
  • Pink eye
  • Skin infection (early-stage, when appropriate)
  • Dental pain bridge care (dentist still required for the underlying problem)
  • Other gradual-onset issues that fit text-based urgent care

Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room for:

  • Chest pain or pressure, especially with arm or jaw pain
  • Trouble breathing or shortness of breath at rest
  • Sudden severe headache unlike anything before
  • Signs of stroke: face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty
  • High fever with stiff neck or altered consciousness
  • Severe abdominal pain, especially lower right
  • Heavy bleeding that won’t stop
  • Broken bones, deep lacerations, head trauma
  • Anything that feels genuinely dangerous or is getting worse fast

When in doubt about severity, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. NPCWoods does not replace emergency care. No facility is named here on purpose — use the closest appropriate emergency option for you.
