What it cannot replace

A dentist still needs to see many teeth. Drainage that needs a procedure. A broken tooth that needs to be opened. Something that has to come out. I cannot drill, pull, or x-ray by text. If you need that door, go there.

When to skip the text and go in

Trouble breathing. Swelling that is closing an eye or spreading down the neck. High fever and you look sick. Uncontrolled bleeding. Chest pain. Call 911 for emergency symptoms. Do not text through those.
