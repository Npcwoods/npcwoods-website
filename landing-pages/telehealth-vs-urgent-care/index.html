No mystery, no runaround

How it actually works.

  1. 01

    Tell us what is going on

    A few plain-language questions from your phone. No waiting room and no calls to make.

  2. 02

    A licensed provider reviews it

    Chris reviews the visit and may follow up with questions before deciding what is appropriate.

  3. 03

    Get clear next steps

    You receive guidance about what to do next, what to watch for, and when to seek in-person care.

What the $59 covers

  • A review by a licensed nurse practitioner
  • A written plan with clear next steps
  • Follow-up questions about the same visit
  • Prescription routing when medically appropriate

What needs in-person care

  • Emergency symptoms such as chest pain, trouble breathing, or stroke signs — call 911
  • Controlled substances
  • Problems that need an exam, imaging, or lab testing
  • Serious or quickly worsening symptoms

We would rather direct you to the right level of care than take your money for something we cannot safely handle online.
