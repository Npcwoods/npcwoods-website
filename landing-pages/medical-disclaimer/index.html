General Information Only

The information provided on this website (npcwoods.com) is for general informational purposes only. It is not intended as, and should not be considered, medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider regarding any medical condition or treatment options.

No Emergency Services

NPCWoods Telemedicine does not provide emergency medical services. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, including but not limited to chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, or signs of a stroke, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room immediately. Do not use this website or our telehealth services for emergencies.

Telehealth Limitations

Telehealth consultations have inherent limitations compared to in-person medical visits. These include, but are not limited to:

  • The inability to perform a physical examination
  • Reliance on patient-reported symptoms and medical history
  • Potential technology-related disruptions
  • The possibility that your condition may require diagnostic testing, imaging, or in-person evaluation that cannot be provided through telehealth

Your NPCWoods provider will refer you to in-person care whenever clinically indicated.

Prescribing Practices

NPCWoods providers prescribe medications only when clinically appropriate, based on established evidence-based clinical guidelines. Key prescribing policies include:

  • No controlled substances (Schedule II-V) are prescribed through our platform
  • Prescriptions are sent to the pharmacy of your choice
  • Your provider will only prescribe what is safe and appropriate for your specific situation
  • Not every consultation will result in a prescription — clinical judgment determines the appropriate course of action
  • If a prescription is not appropriate for your situation, your provider will recommend alternative care options, including referral to in-person evaluation

Individual Results May Vary

Treatment outcomes vary from person to person based on individual health factors, the severity and nature of the condition, adherence to treatment plans, and other variables. The information and treatment descriptions on this website represent general expectations and should not be interpreted as guarantees of specific results.
