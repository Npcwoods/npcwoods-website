How it works

A simple visit, reviewed by a clinician.

NPCWoods is built for common minor health concerns where a text-based urgent care visit may be appropriate. You share what is going on, then a real clinician reviews it and explains the next step.

1

Tell us what's going on

Start with a short text. Share symptoms, timing, age, location, allergies, and any safety concerns that matter.

2

A clinician reviews your visit

Your information is reviewed by a licensed clinician. Follow-up questions may be needed before any recommendation is made.

3

You receive guidance and next steps

You get plainspoken guidance. If medication is appropriate, that decision is made after clinical review.

Important safety note

This service is not for emergencies.

Some symptoms need in-person evaluation right away. If something feels severe, unsafe, or rapidly worsening, seek emergency care or call 911.

Do not use this service for these symptoms.

  • Chest pain
  • Trouble breathing
  • Severe abdominal pain
  • Pregnancy-related emergencies
  • Severe allergic reaction
  • Confusion, fainting, or severe weakness
  • Symptoms needing in-person evaluation

What a text visit can do.

  • Review common minor health concerns
  • Ask follow-up safety questions
  • Explain when in-person care is safer
  • Recommend next steps when clinically appropriate
