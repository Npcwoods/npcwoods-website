Last stop, you learned the honest side effects. Now let's walk through month one together, and remember, it's a test drive, not a race.

The short version

  • Your first dose is a starter, and its job is to help your gut get used to it.
  • Pick one shot day, and keep it the same each week.
  • Your level builds up over about a month, like a bathtub filling.
  • Month one is about fit, not results, so tell me how you feel.
The starter dose

Your first dose is a starter.

Think about new boots. You wouldn't run a race in them on day one, so you break them in first.

The first dose works the same way. It's low on purpose, because its job is to help your gut get used to the medicine. It isn't meant to do the heavy lifting.[2][4]

Most plans stay on the starter dose for 4 weeks. After that, we talk about the next step together.[1][2]

Why it mattersStarting low usually means fewer tummy troubles. Slow and steady really does win here.[1][6]

The bathtub

Your level fills up like a tub.

Picture a bathtub. Each shot pours water into the tub, and your body is the drain that lets some back out.

One shot lasts about a week, so the tub never empties before your next shot. Week by week, the water rises, and in about a month it levels off. That's called steady state.[1][2]

Why it mattersWeek one may feel different from week four, and that's normal. Your tub is still filling up.

Shot day

Pick your shot day.

  1. Pick a day you'll remember. Sunday? Taco Tuesday? It's your call, as long as you keep the same day each week.
  2. Pick a spot. You can use your belly, the front of your thigh, or the back of your upper arm.[1][2]
  3. Switch spots each week. The same area is fine, just not the exact same spot.
  4. Any time works. Morning or night is fine, with or without food.[1][2]
  5. Missed a dose? Text me, because the rule is different for each medicine. Don't double up.

Why it mattersA steady shot day keeps your level steady, and switching spots is kinder to your skin.

Week by week

What month one often feels like.

Every body is different, but here's what I hear a lot.

Week 1

Your first shot. Some people feel a bit queasy a day or two later, and some feel nothing. Both are normal.

Week 2

You may notice you fill up faster, so small plates start to make sense.

Week 3

Your routine starts to click, and shot day feels like no big deal.

Week 4

Your tub is close to steady, so we check in. Next step? We decide together.

The big ideaMonth one is about fit, not results. The real question is whether this medicine is a good match for your body.

Eat to feel good

Eat for comfort and muscle.

You may eat less now, so try to make each bite count.

Protein first

Eggs, fish, chicken, beans, or Greek yogurt. Eat your protein before the rest of your plate.

Small plates

Stop when you're almost full, because a stuffed stomach and a slow stomach don't mix.

Sip all day

Water keeps your gut moving and keeps your kidneys happy.

Fiber helps

Veggies, fruit, and beans help you go. Add them slowly.

Go easy on grease

Fried food sits in your stomach the longest, so it's a top queasy trigger.

Move your muscles

Do strength moves a few times a week. Squats and wall push-ups count.[7]

Why it mattersWhen people lose weight, some of it can be muscle. Eating protein and doing strength moves can help you keep more of it.[7]

Your notes

Jot it down. Then text me.

A quick note each day helps us both, so keep it short and simple.

  • How your tummy feels
  • What you ate when you felt off
  • How much water you drank
  • Bathroom habits
  • Your energy and mood
  • Shot day and spot

Text me before your first dose step. Together, we'll decide if it's time or if your gut needs a little longer.

Heads upRed flags don't wait for a text. Learn them on the side effects page.

Quick questions

You asked. I answered.

Why don't I feel much in week one?

The starter dose is low on purpose, and your level is still filling up, like a tub.

What if I miss a dose?

Text me, because the rule is different for each medicine. Don't double up.

Can I change my shot day?

Often, yes, but you need some space between doses. The rule depends on your medicine, so text me first.

Do I take it with food?

You don't need to. You can take it any time of day, with or without food.
