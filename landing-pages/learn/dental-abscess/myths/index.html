Myths vs facts

This is the Saturday-night pile. I hear these every week. I am going to knock them down in the same order the series taught you.

Fact beats uncle A drain is not done
You
My uncle said if it drains, it is over.
Chris
Your uncle described a pressure valve. The pipe is still open. Fact beats uncle.

The pile

  • Myth: If I ignore it, it will wall off and disappear. Fact: The pocket can go quiet and still spread later. MedlinePlus treats an abscess as something that needs care, not a wait-and-see hobby.
  • Myth: Salt water will cure it. Fact: A rinse is comfort. It is not source control.
  • Myth: If it drains, I am done. Fact: A gum boil that pops is a leak, not a repair.
  • Myth: A text visit replaces a dentist. Fact: I can be a bridge for some people. I cannot drill or pull.
  • Myth: Medicine always gets sent. Fact: A consult does not guarantee a prescription.
  • Myth: If the pain stops, the tooth healed. Fact: A dead nerve can go quiet while germs keep working at the root tip.
  • Myth: Oil, garlic, or whiskey kills the pocket. Fact: Those make breath interesting. They do not clean a canal.
  • Myth: I can crush leftover pills from a friend. Fact: That is a bad idea and I will not help you do it. Your story may not fit that bottle.

The true ending of this series

A dental abscess is a balloon your body built around germs. The door was a hole, a crack, or a gum pocket. The feeling is pressure. The map is tooth, gum, or spreading face. Lookalikes steal the scene. Red flags mean the ER. Care is source first, helper medicine second, and only when it fits. I can text with some of you. A dentist still has to fix the house. If you skip the house, the balloon comes back.

If you want the $59 text visit, and you think you fit, text me. If you need the ER, go. If you can get a dentist today, go there. I would rather you pick the right door than pick my door.

Questions I get

Is it safe to wait if I am scared of the dentist?

Fear is real. Waiting on a spreading infection is worse. Tell the dentist you are scared. Use the ER if red flags show up. Text me if you fit a bridge and need a plan in words.

Do you promise the pain will stop?

No. I do not promise results. I promise an honest read on whether text care is safe.

Where do I start if I am still not sure?

Read the red flag stop again. If you are not on that list, read dentist vs text. Then text me or call a dentist. The offer page is the short version of the visit.
