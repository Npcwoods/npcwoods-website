Dental abscess series · Stop 7

What care looks like

Care has two jobs. Job one is to open the pocket and fix the source. Job two is to calm germs while that happens. Job one is a dentist. Job two is sometimes a medicine I can send to your pharmacy. Job two never replaces job one.

You
Can you just send something so I can skip the dentist?
Chris
I can talk about a bridge. I will not sell you a skip. The hole is still there. Germs move back in if nobody fixes the house.

Source control is the real treatment

Dentists treat the source. They may numb you, open the tooth, let the pus out, clean the nerve room, and seal it later. That path is a root canal. Or they take the tooth out if it cannot be saved. Sometimes they make a small cut in the gum to drain a pocket.

The American Association of Endodontists is blunt about this: an abscessed tooth needs dental treatment. Medicine alone is not the finish line. I agree. I say it in kitchen words. You cannot mop a flood and leave the pipe open.

Where medicine fits

An antibiotic your clinician may send to your pharmacy is a helper. It can calm spreading germs in people who fit that plan. It does not clean the dead nerve. It does not close the cavity. The CDC keeps teaching the same idea across infections: use these medicines when they help, and do not treat them like magic.

A consult does not guarantee a prescription. If you do not fit, I will say so. If you need the ER, I will say so. If you need a dentist today and no medicine yet, I will say that too.

I will not name products on this page. Google health ads and, more important, good care both fail when a landing page turns into a shopping list.

Comfort that does not pretend to cure

  • Salt water rinses can make the gum feel cleaner. They do not empty the basement.
  • A cold pack on the cheek can take the edge off swelling you can see.
  • Soft food on the other side keeps you fed.
  • Store-shelf pain medicine, if you already use it safely, can take the drum down a notch. It does not kill the pocket.

Time

If a bridge is appropriate, people often feel a little better in a day or two. If you are worse, if swelling grows, or if red flags show up, the plan changes. Text me back if we already started a visit. Go to the ER if the airway list shows up.

Questions I get

Will you always send medicine?

No. A consult does not guarantee a prescription. I send something only when it is appropriate and you are in a state I can treat.

If I start medicine, can I cancel the dentist?

No. Medicine is a bridge. The source is still there. Canceling the dentist is how this comes back.

Can you pull the tooth by text?

No. I do not have a chair, an X-ray, or forceps. I can help some people get comfortable enough to reach a dentist.
