Short answer

A licensed nurse practitioner can prescribe by text in North Carolina when the clinical story fits text care. Chris Woods, FNP-C/ACNP-BC, reads every text himself. No video. No app.

Who is licensed

Chris Woods, FNP-C/ACNP-BC, holds a North Carolina nurse practitioner license. NPI 1285125468. You can look the license up on the North Carolina Board of Nursing verification page.

North Carolina practice at NPCWoods includes the collaborative agreement the state requires. The person who reads the text is still Chris.

What a North Carolina text visit can cover

The usual visits are straightforward problems that can be handled from a history: a bladder infection, a sinus infection that has gone on long enough, strep-pattern sore throat, a tooth infection that needs a bridge until the dentist, and similar common issues.

You text (480) 639-4722, say you are in North Carolina, and describe what started, what is worse, your allergies, and your pharmacy. If treatment is appropriate, the prescription goes to that pharmacy. The pharmacy bill is separate from the $59.

  • You are physically in North Carolina during the visit
  • The story is safe to sort by text
  • Same nurse practitioner every time
  • $59. You only pay if he can treat you

What this visit will not do

A prescription is not automatic. If text care is the wrong tool, Chris says so and you are not charged.

NPCWoods does not prescribe controlled substances. It is not an emergency service. Chest pain, trouble breathing, or a story that is getting dangerous belongs with 911 or an ER, not a text.

A tooth visit is a bridge. A dentist still has to treat the tooth. Antibiotics do not fix the decay.

Where you have to be

The license follows your body, not your area code. You need to be in North Carolina, or in another state where Chris is licensed, at the time of the visit. Those states are Arizona, Colorado, Georgia, Idaho, Iowa, Montana, Nevada, New Mexico, North Carolina, Oregon, and Utah.

More on the practice in this state is on the North Carolina telemedicine page. Licenses are listed on the credentials page.
