Know the lane

Helpful by text. Honest about the limits.

Dental pain can come from several causes. The first job is choosing the safest next step.

A text evaluation may help when

  • You have new tooth or gum pain
  • You notice mild, localized swelling
  • You need help deciding how quickly to seek dental care
  • You can answer follow-up questions clearly

Hands-on dental care is still needed for

  • Repairing decay or a damaged tooth
  • Drainage, imaging, fillings, root canals, or extraction
  • Pain after dental work that needs an exam
  • Symptoms that are severe, spreading, or unclear
Do not wait for a text reply

Get immediate help for emergency warning signs.

  • Trouble breathing or swallowing
  • Rapidly spreading face, jaw, or neck swelling
  • Swelling near the eye
  • Confusion, fainting, or severe weakness
  • High fever with rapidly worsening symptoms
  • Any symptom that feels life-threatening

Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room now.

Text care does not replace the dental work.

The cause still matters. Tooth and gum problems often need hands-on dental care to fix what started the pain. Chris helps you understand the immediate lane while you arrange the right dental follow-through.
