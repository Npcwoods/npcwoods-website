Mount Rainier

14,410 feet does not care about your sinuses. A lobby will take the afternoon anyway.

Rainier is an active volcano and the most glaciated peak in the contiguous United States. Paradise meadows in one season. Sunrise on the other shoulder. You already spent the drive getting here. Facial pressure after heater air, then thin, cold air, is a boring story. It is also a common one.

You can sit in a clinic waiting room and watch the clock, or you can text from the trailhead and keep the mountain in front of you. $59. No video. Chris Woods is licensed in Washington, ARNP.AP.61670388-NP. You must be in Washington during the visit.

Wait here

Paradise. Sunrise. Wildflowers or snow, depending on the month. The peak sitting there like it owns the sky.

VS
Not there

A clinic waiting room. Plastic chairs. Someone coughing two seats over. The mountain still out there without you.

This page is for the uncomplicated sinus squeeze after the drive, not for altitude emergencies, chest pain, or trouble breathing. If the head pressure is paired with confusion, fainting, or you cannot catch your breath, this is the wrong door.

Text what day you are on, what the pressure is doing, and a pharmacy that is actually on your way back down. When a prescription is appropriate, it can go there. When it is not, you get the honest support plan.
