Can strep throat really be diagnosed via telehealth without a rapid test?
Yes — for adults. Clinical prediction tools (like the Centor or McIsaac score) allow providers to assess strep likelihood based on symptoms alone and guide antibiotic decisions. For children, our NP will discuss what is most appropriate based on your child’s age and presentation.
What antibiotic is used for strep throat?
Penicillin or amoxicillin is the first-line treatment for Group A Strep. Both are inexpensive generics available at any Mesa-area pharmacy, often under $10 with a discount card.
My child has an ear infection — can telehealth help?
For children over age 2 presenting with clear ear infection symptoms, our NP can provide evaluation and treatment guidance. For children under 2, or if symptoms are severe, in-person evaluation is standard of care — your NP will let you know.
What if my symptoms turn out not to be strep or an ear infection?
