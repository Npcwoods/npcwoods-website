Frequently Asked Questions — Sinus Infection in Tempe, AZ

Can a telehealth provider diagnose a sinus infection without an in-person exam?
Yes. Acute bacterial sinusitis is diagnosed clinically — based on symptom duration, character, and pattern — not through imaging. Your NP is trained to distinguish bacterial sinusitis from viral upper respiratory infections and allergies, and will prescribe accordingly.
Do all sinus infections need antibiotics?
No — and a good NP will tell you the truth about that. Most acute sinusitis cases begin virally and resolve on their own. Our NP will assess your specific presentation and recommend antibiotics only when clinically appropriate.
What antibiotic is typically used for sinus infections?
Amoxicillin-clavulanate (Augmentin) is a common first-line choice for bacterial sinusitis, though your NP will select based on your history and any allergies. Most options are inexpensive generics.
How long does it take to feel better after starting treatment?
Most patients with bacterial sinusitis notice improvement within 48–72 hours of starting an appropriate antibiotic.
